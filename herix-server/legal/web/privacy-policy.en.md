## 1. Information We Collect, Purposes, and Methods

We follow the principle of "minimum necessary" and collect only the information required to provide specific features. You may decline to provide "optional" information, but the corresponding features may then be unavailable.

| Type of information | Purpose and use | Method | Required/Optional |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email address** | Account registration, login, password reset, and important notices | You provide it | Required |
| **Nickname** | Display within the platform | You provide it | Required |
| **Contact details (phone number / messaging app ID, etc.)** | So that brands can contact you directly within a task | You provide it | Optional |
| **Social media accounts and follower/friend counts** (Instagram, TikTok, LINE, WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, X, etc.) | Displaying your influence; brand evaluation and task matching | You provide it | Optional |
| **Work-eligibility declaration (e.g., residency status)** | Confirming side-work eligibility in certain regions (e.g., Japan) | You declare it | Required when receiving bank payouts |
| **Payout and settlement information** (bank account, payout account, etc.) | Paying and reconciling your rewards | You provide it | Required when requesting a withdrawal |
| **Task and transaction records** (applications, referral codes, conversions, settlement records) | Providing core services, calculating and paying rewards, reconciliation and dispute handling | Generated as you use the Service | Required |
| **Device and log information** (access time, operation logs, necessary technical identifiers) | Securing accounts and transactions, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and improvement | Generated automatically as you use the Service | Required |

We do not collect information unrelated to the above purposes, nor do we force you to provide optional information.

## 2. How We Use Information

1. To provide core services such as account login, task application, promotion matching, and reward settlement;
2. To display information to partner brands to the extent necessary for task matching (see Section 4);
3. For identity verification, security, and fraud prevention;
4. To send you service-related notices within the scope of your consent;
5. To comply with laws and regulations, fulfill compliance obligations, and handle disputes.

We do not use your personal information for purposes beyond the above; where we need to, we will obtain your consent again.

---

## 3. How We Store Information

1. **Storage method**: Your personal information is stored on secure servers protected by measures such as encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and access controls.
2. **Retention period**: We retain it only for as long as necessary to achieve the collection purpose, after which we delete or anonymize it. Where retention is required by law (e.g., accounting retention of settlement records), such requirements apply.

---

## 4. How We Share, Transfer, or Disclose Information

We do **not sell** your personal information. We share it only in the following cases:

1. **Sharing with partner brands**: To carry out a task you have applied to, we display to the corresponding brand the contact details and social media account information you provided for that task. No other information is shared.
2. **Sharing with service providers**: To settle rewards, we may provide necessary payout information to payment/settlement providers; to provide cloud services, data is stored with cloud providers. Such parties may only process information within our instructions.
3. **Disclosure required by law**: We may disclose information where required by law or by lawful requests from judicial or administrative authorities.
4. **Transfer**: We transfer information only in cases such as mergers or acquisitions, requiring the recipient to continue to abide by this Policy; otherwise we will obtain your consent again.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information and may exercise them through the contact channel in Section 8:

1. **Access and correction**: Access and correct your account and social media account information (most can be changed yourself on the "Profile" screen);
2. **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal information in accordance with applicable law;
3. **Withdrawal of consent**: Withdraw consent you previously gave (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal);
4. **Account deregistration**: Request deregistration of your Herix account, after which we will delete or anonymize your personal information (except where retention is required by law).

We will respond to your request within a reasonable period.

## 6. Protection of Minors

The Service is intended for adults with full legal capacity. We do not provide the Service to minors, nor do we knowingly collect minors' personal information. If you are a minor, please do not use the Service.
